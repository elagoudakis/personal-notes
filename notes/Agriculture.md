# Agriculture

**Summary**: Notes on agriculture and crop monitoring, including field mapping from Earth observation data.
**Last updated**: 2026-11-18

---

- [agribound](https://github.com/montimaj/agribound): A [[Python]] toolkit for delineating agricultural field boundaries from [[Remote_Sensing]] imagery (Landsat, Sentinel-2, HLS, NAIP, SPOT). It uses [[Deep_Learning]] models (Delineate-Anything, Fields of The World, DINOv3, Prithvi), clustering of [[Embeddings]], and optional SAM 2 refinement. *Keywords*: field boundaries, delineation, satellite imagery, foundation models, Google Earth Engine, Python package
