# Climate Change

**Summary**: Notes on climate change, its impacts and related hazards such as floods, and datasets for studying them.
**Last updated**: 2026-11-18

---

- [Groundsource: A Dataset of Flood Events from News](https://zenodo.org/records/18647054): An open [[Data]] set (Zenodo, Feb 2026) of about 2.6M historical flood events in more than 150 countries, extracted from news articles. It is a single Parquet file under the CC BY 4.0 license. *Keywords*: floods, flood events, news, open dataset, Parquet, Zenodo
