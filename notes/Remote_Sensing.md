# Remote Sensing

**Summary**: Notes on satellite and aerial Earth observation: data products, methods, and tools.
**Last updated**: 2026-11-18

---

- [First thermal images from Sentinel-3C radiometer](https://www.esa.int/articles/about-us/directorates/earth-observation-programmes/copernicus/sentinel-3/first-thermal-images-from-sentinel-3c-radiometer): ESA (Nov 2026) shows the first thermal and SWIR images from the SLSTR radiometer on Sentinel-3C, which launched on 15 Sep 2026. The images cover Western Australia and Iberia and show surface temperatures, vegetation and wildfire burn scars linked to heatwaves and [[Climate_Change]]. *Keywords*: Sentinel-3C, SLSTR, thermal infrared, land surface temperature, wildfires, Copernicus
- [The Technical Debt of Earth Embedding Products](https://cloudnativegeo.org/blog/2026/02/the-technical-debt-of-earth-embedding-products/): Isaac Corley (Feb 2026) compares Earth [[Embeddings]] products such as Clay, Major TOM, Tessera, AlphaEarth and Presto. He argues that each one ships its own format, grid and CRS assumptions, which forces custom integration code. He calls for cloud-native [[Data]] formats (GeoParquet, COG, Zarr), benchmarks, provenance and quantization. *Keywords*: earth embeddings, geospatial foundation models, technical debt, cloud-native formats, GeoParquet, quantization
